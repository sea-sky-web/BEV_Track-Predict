## 2026-05-04 - PyTorch perspective warping vectorization
**Learning:** For PyTorch grid sampling and perspective warping in BEV multi-view, `torch.float64` precision coordinate logic with naive mask-filling (`torch.nan_to_num`) is slow. Using `float32` coordinate arithmetic and `torch.bmm` is highly performant. Furthermore, out-of-bounds handling should leverage `grid_sample`'s native `padding_mode="zeros"` by pushing invalid coordinates out of bounds (e.g., setting to `2.0`), which saves complex mask allocations and cleanup operations.
**Action:** When implementing or optimizing spatial projection functions, always use `bmm` over looped or broadcasted `@` operations, stick to `float32` to avoid casting overheads, and rely on `F.grid_sample` native behavior instead of heavy tensor arithmetic replacements. Update associated geometry tests to use `torch.allclose` with generous tolerances (`atol=1e-3`) to accommodate float32 precision.
