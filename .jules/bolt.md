## 2024-06-16 - Vectorize Multi-View and Optimize Grid Sample
**Learning:** PyTorch `grid_sample` is highly performant and handles out-of-bounds coordinates automatically. Manual out-of-bounds penalties (like `torch.where` or `nan_to_num`) in `warp_perspective_torch` are slow. Furthermore, iterating over multiple views (`V` dimension) with a Python `for` loop in `MVDetLikeNet` severely underutilizes the GPU compared to collapsing the batch and view dimensions (`B * V`) into a single batch dimension for backbone processing.
**Action:** Always prefer flattening batch and temporal/view dimensions (`B * V, C, H, W`) before passing tensors into CNN backbones to maximize vectorization. For coordinate warping, push invalid points out of the `[-1, 1]` grid space (e.g. `2.0`) and let `F.grid_sample(padding_mode='zeros')` natively handle clipping rather than trying to mask values.
