## 2026-05-04 - [Optimize MVDet Multi-View Projection & Fusion]
**Learning:** In a multi-view BEV detection pipeline, sequentially looping over view dimensions (e.g., `for v in range(V)`) during feature extraction and projection creates a major performance bottleneck due to lack of vectorization. Additionally, manually masking out-of-bounds coordinates during perspective warping with `torch.isfinite` and `torch.nan_to_num` is slow and complex. `torch.nn.functional.grid_sample` can natively and efficiently handle out-of-bounds invalid values via `padding_mode="zeros"`.
**Action:** Always flatten batch and view dimensions (e.g., `x.view(B * V, C, H, W)`) to leverage PyTorch's native parallel batch processing. In projection/warping logic, use `float32` arithmetic, `torch.bmm` for batched matrix multiplication, and push invalid homogeneous coordinates explicitly out of normalized bounds (e.g., `invalid = z <= 1e-6; x = torch.where(invalid, 2.0, x)`) to rely entirely on `grid_sample`'s native padding mechanism.
